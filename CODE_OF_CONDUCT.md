# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive feedback, accept responsibility, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward other contributors.

Harassment, insults, threats, unwanted attention, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
